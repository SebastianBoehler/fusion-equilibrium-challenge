# nn-008 — full-data neural model with spatial Thomson features

## Why the incumbent family is capped

The public development leaderboard (read 8 October 2026 from the Codabench API,
phase 29580) shows about fifty entries at DIII-D S ≥ 0.993. The top cluster is
S ≈ 0.9963–0.9964 with flux R² 0.9999, q95/βN R² ≈ 0.998, D_LCFS ≈ 0.008 and
consistency ≈ 0.987. The organizers' own entry is 0.9951. Submission 968596 is
S 0.9521 with flux R² 0.9853 and consistency 0.8405.

| Component (weight) | 968596 | Top cluster | Weighted gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Flux R² (0.55) | 0.9853 | 0.9999 | 0.0080 |
| q95/βN R² (0.15) | 0.9644 | 0.998 | 0.0050 |
| 1 − D_LCFS (0.10) | 0.9745 | 0.992 | 0.0018 |
| Consistency (0.20) | 0.8405 | 0.987 | 0.0293 |

The gap is not regularization or scalar-head choice. The quadratic ridge is a
fixed 594-term basis that is linear in the flux pixels, and its Thomson input is
six position-free quantiles per system. The learning curve 256 → 1,024 shots
gave +0.030 S, but extrapolating that family does not reach flux R² 0.9999.

## Review of the earlier reasoning

Sound and kept:

- Shot-level splits, a seeded 256-shot development set, a locked 128-shot
  confirmation set, and no fitting on public inputs.
- Exact use of the organizer scorer, upload-precision scoring, the Ip time-axis
  fix, and checks that features never read EFIT columns.
- Separating scalar-head changes from flux changes.

Where it constrained performance:

- **Model class.** Every flux model was a linear map from a fixed basis. The
  organizer baselines and the leaderboard point to neural models on the full
  training release.
- **Data use.** At most 1,024 of 7,041 training shots were used.
- **Thomson geometry discarded.** The core system is a vertical chord at
  R = 1.94 m covering Z ≈ 0.03 → 0.69–0.83 m, which crosses the plasma top. The
  tangential system covers the outboard midplane at R ≈ 1.67 → 2.06/2.27 m.
  Where Te and ne fall off along these chords locates the boundary. That is
  direct evidence for κ, Z_axis and volume, the weakest consistency terms.
  Quantiles throw that position away.
- **No actuator history.** Vessel eddy currents scale with dI/dt, and EFIT
  includes them. The incumbent sees only instantaneous currents.
- **Process overhead.** Most wall-clock went into receipts, resumable scoring,
  file-handle failures and a one-hour CPU job cap. Data for these experiments
  fits on one GPU node.

Rule notes (Rules page, read 8 October): label-free use of test *inputs*
(normalization, adaptation) is explicitly permitted. Training must rebuild in
≤ 24 A100-hours. Final-phase entries need a public reproduction package. The
Hugging Face pointer submission avoids the ~1 h direct upload.

## Method

- `participant/rich.py` — 227 features per EFIT frame, from allowed inputs only.
  For each of the 21 currents: a ±1 ms mean, short and long slopes (±1–5 ms and
  ±5–20 ms window differences) and a 20–100 ms lag. Three timing features. Core
  Te/ne/pe on 18 fixed Z points and tangential Te/ne/pe on 13 fixed R points,
  each with a coverage mask, so all chord layouts share one feature space. Eight
  profile summaries per system: peaks, integral, centroid, spread, outer and
  inner dense extent, and coverage. The redaction check passes.
- `participant/nn_prepare.py` — process-parallel extraction into float32 memory
  maps for train (6,641), development (256) and regression (16). The 128
  confirmation shots are neither downloaded nor processed.
- `participant/nn_train.py` — residual MLP to a 256-component PCA flux basis.
  The basis is orthonormal, so the flux loss term equals 1 − R²ψ within the
  subspace. q95/βN heads are standardized; li and the axis position are
  auxiliary targets.
- `participant/fast_score.py` — runs the unmodified organizer functions with a
  process pool. `tests/fast_score_checks.py` verifies its output equals the
  serial `participant.score.composite` exactly and that a perfect prediction
  scores S = 1.0.
- `participant/nn_submit.py` — builds an averaged-ensemble submission with the
  same order-manifest and saved-array validation as `submit_build`.
