# Magnetics test-time adaptation and test-shot matching — 22 October 2026

## Human decision (22 Oct)
The human decided, without asking the organizers, that the anonymised MAST test shots may be
re-identified by matching their released traces to public FAIR-MAST shots. Those shots' public
magnetics (flux loops and pickup coils) may then be used for label-free test-time adaptation.
- FAIR-MAST `equilibrium` and EFIT/CPF products are still never read.
- **This must be disclosed in provenance and in the methods report.**

## Validation design (predeclared)
- **Shots:** non-test FAIR-MAST shots, tuning `subset80` and holdout `holdout80`, 7,365 and
  6,996 frames.
- **Method:** fit on some sensor groups, score on the others (held out from the fit).
- **Metric:** per-channel pooled R² of the plasma contribution, using the harness convention.
- **Gate:** at least +0.02 on held-out groups on both sets, and no group worse by more than 0.01.
- **Code:** `research/mag_tta.py`, `research/mag_tta_eval.py`.
- **Outputs:** `run_data/mag-tta/E2-*.json`.

### E2: per-frame convex blend of 6 profile variants plus a rigid vertical shift
- **Variants:** ALLgf with Te^0.5, 0.75, 1.0 and 1.5, and edge-current shares 0.1 and 0.2.
- **Settings:** λ = 1 pulls the weights toward the base; the shift prior has σ = 3 cm.

| Held-out group | Tuning: base → E2 | Holdout: base → E2 |
|---|---|---|
| Coil loops (fit on CC + probes) | 0.773 → 0.795 (+0.021) | 0.805 → 0.825 (+0.021) |
| CC loops (fit on coil loops + probes) | 0.786 → 0.804 (+0.018) | 0.756 → 0.784 (+0.028) |
| CCBV (fit on loops) | 0.338 → 0.334 (−0.004) | 0.144 → 0.178 (+0.033) |
| OBV (fit on loops) | 0.345 → 0.357 (+0.011) | −0.001 → 0.030 (+0.030) |
| OBR (fit on loops) | 0.306 → 0.456 (+0.149) | 0.268 → 0.380 (+0.112) |

- **Fits:** the mean base weight is 0.75–0.84; dz p10/p50/p90 is −3 / 0 / +1 cm.
- **Reading:** a borderline pass.
  - The holdout clears the gate on every group.
  - On the tuning set, CC misses by 0.002 and CCBV/OBV are below +0.02, but no group degrades
    by more than 0.01.
  - The size is about +0.02 on the magnetics. Its scored effect is unknown, and probably small.

### E3: per-frame vacuum multipole correction (5 polynomial vacuum fields)
- **Rejected.** The polynomial fields extrapolate catastrophically to held-out sensors: on 3
  tuning shots, CC loops R² = −124.

### E4: per-frame current correction in 16 passive-filament clusters (EFIT-style free passive)
- **Rejected.**
- **On held-out sensors** (holdout):
  - CC loops R² −25.9;
  - coil loops −1.37;
  - CCBV −1.31.
- **In sample** (all → all): it fits well, with CC 0.96, CCBV 0.87 and OBV 0.78.
- **Reading:** with these sensors, a per-frame correction to the external field cannot be
  identified. The extra freedom absorbs sensor-specific errors.

## Full index and match verification (23 Oct, night)
- **Index:** 11,492 of 11,573 FAIR-MAST shots. The other 81 failed to download, none of them
  at shot ≥ 26000.
- **Matching:** 853 private-test shots pass the predeclared rule (distance < 0.003, margin > 5×).
- **V1 identity check** (`research/match_verify.py`): total flux-loop R² between the matched
  shot's measured loops and our flux built from the release coil currents.
  - **Threshold:** the p1 of 777 near-certain matches (distance < 0.002), which is 0.558.
  - **Borderline matches** (distance 0.003–0.01) score like the near-certain ones: median R²
    0.97.
  - **Negative control:** 149 deliberate wrong pairings. Median 0.03, but **28% pass** the
    threshold. Repeat shots share nearly identical coil programmes.
  - **Reading:** V1 cannot separate a true match from a repeat shot. The Ip distance can:
    true matches sit about 1–2 kA RMS apart, repeat shots about 8–12 kA.
- **Decision:**
  - Keep the predeclared Ip rule.
  - Use V1 only as a data-quality filter: it drops matches whose loops disagree with the coils.
  - The 0.003–0.01 band is not added.
  - **Result:** `match-private-accepted-b.json` → candidate **TTA-b**.
